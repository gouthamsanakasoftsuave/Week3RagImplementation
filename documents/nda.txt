MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 January 2025
between Northwind Analytics Ltd ("Disclosing Party") and Harbor Legal Services LLP
("Receiving Party").

1. Confidentiality
Confidential Information means all non-public business, technical, and financial information
disclosed by either party, including customer lists, source code, and draft contract terms.
The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely to evaluate a potential commercial
relationship and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
The Receiving Party must protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care
it uses for its own confidential materials, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2. Term
The confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after the last disclosure under this
Agreement.

3. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London have
exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising out of this Agreement.

4. Return of materials
Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or securely destroy all copies of
Confidential Information, except one copy retained solely for legal archive purposes.
