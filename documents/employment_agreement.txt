EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is between Harbor Legal Services LLP (the "Employer") and
Jordan Hale (the "Employee"), starting 1 April 2025.

1. Position
Employee will serve as Senior Contract Analyst, reporting to the Head of Legal Operations.

2. Compensation
Base salary is GBP 72,000 per year, paid monthly in arrears. Employee is eligible for a
discretionary annual bonus of up to 10% of base salary.

3. Confidentiality
Employee shall not disclose Employer client matters or internal playbooks during employment
and for two (2) years after employment ends.

4. Intellectual Property
All work product created by Employee in the course of employment is work made for hire and
belongs exclusively to Employer.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate employment without cause by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.
Employer may terminate immediately for gross misconduct, including unauthorized disclosure of
client files. Upon termination, Employee must return all Employer devices and documents within
five (5) business days.

6. Governing Law
This Employment Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
