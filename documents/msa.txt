MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "MSA") is between Northwind Analytics Ltd ("Provider")
and Cedar Retail Group ("Customer"), effective 15 March 2025.

1. Services
Provider will deliver software implementation and support services as described in each signed
Statement of Work ("SOW"). Each SOW is incorporated into this MSA.

2. Fees
Customer shall pay invoices within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late amounts accrue
interest at 1.5% per month.

3. Limitation of Liability
Except for fraud, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality, each party's total aggregate
liability under this MSA shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12)
months immediately preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages, including lost profits.

4. Indemnification
Provider shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the delivered services infringe
a patent or copyright, provided Customer gives prompt written notice and exclusive control of the
defense.

5. Intellectual Property
Provider retains all intellectual property in its pre-existing tools and platforms. Customer owns
deliverables specifically created for Customer under an SOW, excluding Provider's pre-existing
materials.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this MSA for convenience on sixty (60) days' prior written notice.
Either party may terminate immediately if the other party materially breaches this MSA and fails
to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

7. Governing Law
This MSA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, excluding conflict-of-law rules.
